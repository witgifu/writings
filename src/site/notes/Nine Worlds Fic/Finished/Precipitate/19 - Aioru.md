Aioru is down by the harbour, at a meeting with the very astute Chair of the Port Authority. She's a no-nonsense woman, who Aioru suspects is very carefully using small-vessel exclusions in the legislation to reduce the duties locals are paying. It's technically above board, but Aioru is itching to fix the law.

A messenger comes running into their meeting room. “The _Gold Pearl_ is signalling 'found missing person CM, onboard',” they say, panting slightly, “CM? Anyone you know missing with those initials?”

“Sayo Mdang!” says Aioru, jumping up.

The messenger and the Chair both look confused. “Cliopher Mdang? I saw him just yesterday at the library,” says the Chair.

Now it is Aioru's turn to look confused, “Hands of the Emperor? Head of the Imperial Bureaucratic Service? Secretary in Chief of the Offices of the Lords of State? Lives in Solaara?”

“Oh! Kip Mdang,” says the Chair, “Although I didn't know he had all those fancy titles. Or that he was missing.”

“I will need to report this to his Radiancy immediately,” says Aioru, “My apologies, for having to leave so soon. I am sure we can finish the rest of this by correspondence. Do you know how long until the _Gold Pearl_ makes port?”

“Probably two hours?” says the messenger, “Could only just read the flags through the telescope.”

“Thank you,” says Aioru, and dashes from the room, and out of the building. Weaving through the crowds in the business district, he spots an off-duty Pikabe.

“Guard Pikabe?” says Aioru, realising as he does that he is perhaps not as fit as he used to be. Running is hard.

“Yes…,” says Pikabe with a sigh, looking around, “Oh! Sayo Airou!”

“I know you're not on duty… but I've just heard Sayo Mdang has been found, he's on a ship, and it's arriving in about two hours.”

Pikabe's eyebrows shoot up. “I'll run and tell Commander Omo,” he says, “Don't worry.”

“Thank you,” says Aioru, “I owe you.”

Pikabe waves off the thanks as he sets off up the hill. Before long, he's out of sight.

Will his Radiancy wait at the palace for Sayo Mdang? It seems unlikely, Aioru thinks his lord will probably want to be at the port when Sayo Mdang disembarks. He takes the main route up to the palace, just in case he needs to turn around at some point and follow his lord back to the port.

When Aioru makes it to the palace, it is not abuzz with the news as he had expected. Spotting Saya Kindraa, Aioru wanders over.

“What have I missed?” he asks. 

“Nothing much, just that the seneschal was finally arrested for embezzlement,” she says with a sigh, “He was lifting pearls from the tithe, can you believe? Apparently nobody in Solaara was comparing the counts.”

Aioru shakes his head. Such a risky way to earn a few hundred valiants.

“Commander Omo, and Lord Conju?” he asks, looking around.

“Both with his Radiancy,” she answers, “The audit team has given an initial report to the legal team. So perhaps they are discussing further arrests.”

Aioru sighs, and heads to the princess's suite, which has been taken over by his Radiancy. Or perhaps rather by Lord Conju, who Aioru has heard complaining about the decorative preferences of the previous occupant. Apparently it included a distastefully large amount of sandalwood fretwork.

“We shall walk down to meet the ship,” says his Radiancy, voice carrying through the open doors. 

Aioru stops beside the outer guards, listening.

“Very well, my lord, we will prepare the sedan chair—” says Lord Conju, sounding very gentle.

The guards wave Aioru inside. Startled, he follows their direction.

“No,” says his Radiancy, interrupting the Groom of the Chamber, although his voice is as calm as always, “We will walk.”

Turning the corner, Aioru can see Lord Conju making a deep bow of apology. The man has performed miracles since Sayo Mdang went missing, it seems cruel that just as he is found, his Radiancy rebukes Lord Conju rather than thanking him.

But it seems his Radiancy has had the same thought. “My dear Conju,” he says, “We only wish to see for ourselves, as soon as possible, that Cliopher is well. Surely you do also, please come with us.”

Aioru admits to himself that he really is just eavesdropping now. He squares his shoulders, and walks into the sitting room. His lord is perched on the edge of an ornately carved chair, a selection of delicacies sits ignored on the table beside him. Aioru falls into the obeisance, and is waved up startlingly quickly.

“Do you know any more, Sayo Aioru?” asks his Radiancy.

“My lord,” says Aioru, putting on his best reporting voice, “The ship the _Gold Pearl_ was seen making for Gorjo City. She was flying the message 'found missing person CM aboard'. The Port Authority estimated she would dock in about two hours when the message was read. They are not aware of anyone else missing who the message might refer to.”

Commander Omo salutes.

“No more than six guards, Commander,” says his Radiancy, “The city has been very cooperative, and we don't wish to imply we don't trust them.”

They make it out of the palace in surprisingly good time. Aioru hasn't been dismissed, so he tags along at the back. Perhaps his Radiancy will want to notify the Palace of Stars as soon as they have confirmation that Sayo Mdang has returned. He will need Aioru to write the letter.

The city is busy, but not crowded. Everyone stops to watch the Emperor pass, performing the obeisance without skill but with good humour. His Radiancy waves, but doesn't slow at all. The pace rather reminds Aioru of Sayo Mdang on the way to a meeting.

Lord Conju manages to convince his Radiancy to wait at the Port Authority building, rather than actually on the pier. Aioru doesn't catch the discussion, but possibly there are comments about needing to give people space to do their work. Both the first and second floors of the building have beautiful large glass windows looking out at the ships.

His Radiancy paces along the windows, his robes swirling at each end. It is, perhaps, slightly faster than his usual pacing when dictating. The two guards in the room stand stoically, Commander Omo is outside somewhere negotiating with the Port Authority. Lord Conju stands against the wall, as if he is part of the furniture. Aioru does his best to imitate the Groom.

When the large merchant vessel, which must surely be the _Gold Pearl_, rounds the headland, his Radiancy continues pacing, although not taking his eyes from it. Aioru wonders that his lord hasn't made for the pier. But it is clear over the next while that it is simply that his Radiancy knows how long the process takes for the ship to berth to the point where passengers can disembark.

Finally, a gangway is wheeled along the pier. His Radiancy stops pacing, watching it creep up and be swung and tied into place. The pier is swarming with people, as a crane swings into place and stevedores run up the ramp. It would not be safe, for his Radiancy to go out among so many busy workers. They could easily bump into him, and risk immolation.

His Radiancy must realise this too. He resumes pacing. “Conju,” he says, turning at the end, “Please would you go down to meet Cliopher and tell him…”

He pauses, both talking and pacing, to stare at the ship again. “Tell him…,” he tries again, and then takes a long, slow breath in and out. “That we do not blame him, that we are happy he is safe, and…” 

He reaches out one perfectly manicured finger to touch the glass. With a very small voice, that sounds nothing like Aioru has ever heard from the Emperor before, he says, “His protocol failed to take into account that I couldn't cope without him.”

“Of course, my lord,” says Lord Conju, and bows himself out of the room.

Aioru, and presumably his Radiancy, watch the elegant nobleman stride through the crowd of workmen like he has every right to be there. Up the gangway, and onto the ship. Moments later a small group appears. Lord Conju is leading, with Ser Rhodin beside him. Following them is Sayo Mdang.

His Radiancy lets out a small gasp, pressing his palm up against the glass. It feels wrong for Aioru to be here, as if he is witnessing a thing that should be private. But of course, his Radiancy is never actually alone.

There are two others behind Sayo Mdang, one of them is Oginu, the other guard his Radiancy sent. They are all hurrying to the Port Authority building. His Radiancy steps back, away from the window, and turns towards the door. Aioru can see his lord twisting his hands together behind his back.

The guards announce the party, but Sayo Mdang rushes past them before they have finished naming everyone. He drops into the obeisance at the exact prescribed distance. Impatiently, his Radiancy signals for Sayo Mdang to rise.

“Cliopher… are you truly not hurt?” he says, softly.

“I am fine, my lord,” says Sayo Mdang, looking right at his Radiancy, eyes sparkling, still kneeling, “You saved my life, and sent me to my cousin who I love like a brother, who I hadn't heard from since the Fall. And then you sent Rhodin and Oginu to find me, and bring me back to you. Thank you, my lord.”

It is such a shame that his Radiancy can touch no one, and nobody can touch him, thinks Aioru. It is so clear now that Sayo Mdang would hug his Radiancy if he could. But all he can do is offer the same gestures of fealty as everyone else. With everyone here staring at them both.

Aioru coughs gently.

“Yes, Sayo Aioru?” says his Radiancy.

“Shall I inform the Saya Kalikiri and the rest of the Palace of Stars, my lord?” he asks, sounding less nervous than he feels.

“Of course,” says his Radiancy.

“Glorious One,” says the man Aioru doesn't recognise, but with Islander features, “Might I tell Kip's family?”

“Yes, please do,” says his Radiancy.

Ser Rhodin salutes, and his Radiancy turns to him next, “Yes, Ser Rhodin, you and Guard Oginu may report to Commander Omo.”

Then he turns to Lord Conju, “Yes, Cavalier, you can organise the sedan chair.” He is smiling gently, there is no censure, only perhaps a gentle teasing.

Everyone except Sayo Mdang and his Radiancy files out of the room. Even the two inner guards. This surprises Aioru, although he has no reason to challenge it. The guards seem to be trying to make some pretence of a conference with Ser Rhodin about returning to the palace later.

Aioru treks up to the Spire, composing the letter to Saya Kalikiri in his head.
