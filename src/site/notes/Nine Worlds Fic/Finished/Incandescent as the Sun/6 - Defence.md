With the tonic and exercises, Conju could tell that Cliopher's eyesight was once again slowly improving. He stopped clipping doorways with his elbows, and could sometimes do a convincing job of making eye contact across the dining table.

It was the twenty minutes per day of sun that was proving to be the most difficult. Firstly, because Cliopher had so much to do during daylight hours. Secondly, because the gardens were still dangerous in places. Between Cliopher's complete lack of magic sense, and his impaired vision, it was all too easy for him to end up in trouble outside.

Conju had only let him try twice on his own, before quietly looking over Cliopher's schedule. Most of the time he could manage to get away and escort Cliopher himself. When he couldn't, he could usually talk one of Ser Ergaz, Guard Hiscaron, or Guard Omo into accompanying Cliopher.

On this particular day, his Radiancy had taken his time in his private study when Cliopher had an appropriate gap. So Conju met Cliopher at the doors, and they had a pleasant amble together.

Walking arm-in-arm through the gardens probably wasn't helping the rumours that the two of them were a couple. Conju had decided he didn't care. It was only going to be a problem if anyone was jealous, and any potential boyfriend or dalliance who objected to Cliopher was an automatic rejection anyway.

The time outside was pleasant for Conju as well. It was nice to see the sky above, and some gardens were almost tamed enough to be admired. And it was pleasant, to admire growing things, and chat in the fresh air.

There was a small arbour, with jasmine artfully draped over it that Conju particularly liked. Behind the arbour, was a rosemary bush that a gardener was clearly planning to make into some kind of ornamental shape. Conju had stepped through to see if he could identify what the shape might be, when he heard a cutting voice behind him.

“Is this where I find you, Sayo Madon?” said a well-bread drawl.

“My apologies, Lord Frugi, I did not know you were looking for me. I thought our meeting was the day after tomorrow?” said Cliopher.

Conju turned around slowly. Lord Frugi was flanked by a sneering friend, and a manservant who appeared to be at least half-guard.

“It is, Madon. But I needed you earlier, and you couldn't be found. Then I walk past you in the gardens and you don't so much as *nod*. Back in Astandalas I would have had you flogged for that kind of behaviour,” said Lord Frugi. His friend nodded sycophantically.

Conju's palms were itching, wanting to step forward and cut the man down to size. He was clearly one of the old members of the Service who had climbed to the top with bribes and connections rather than any kind of skill. Most of that kind of people had been weeded out by the Fall—all of them had from the Tower—but a few were just smart enough to keep themselves afloat. 

It wasn't as if Conju minded people who were purely decorative (he admired quite a few). Or that he objected to families who sent their children into the Service as a mater of course, or to make good connections. It was _charlatans_ he couldn't stand.

“My apologies, Lord Frugi. I believe I was in the Offices of State for most of the afternoon yesterday, but I did have a number of meetings. I—” Cliopher's explanation was interrupted by Lord Frugi.

With the tone of one explaining something to a child, Lord Frugi said, “I sent for you in the morning.” He continued, and his voice grew quieter, “The page claimed something about you being with the Emperor, but that was clearly a tall tale you gave them to make them go away. I don't appreciate your attitude. Just because you're in Indrogan's little cadre doesn't mean you can get away with the kind of impudence you seem to be making a habit.”

Conju was willing to let Cliopher defend himself. Whoever this Lord Frugi thought he was, he was clearly out of touch with who _Cliopher_ was now.

“The page told you the truth, Lord Frugi. I have the honour of attending his Radiancy every day from the third bell of the morning,” said Cliopher. Respectful, but cool. Conju approved, there was no need to flatter this ego, Frugi seemed to do plenty of that himself.

Lord Frugi's eyes flashed. There must have been some kind of signal, because the manservant reached for something behind himself. Conju also noticed him scanning around to check for the guards. The manservant stepped forward to look threateningly at Cliopher. 

Cliopher would have noticed none of this, Conju was reasonably sure. He could see a full arm-length in front of himself now, but beyond that was mostly blurred. Something like a man reaching for a knife, or taking a small step forward, could easily be missed. It was time to intervene, as much as he disliked it.

“Lord… Frugi, was it?” said Conju, and stepped clearly into the line of sight of all three men. “I hope you are not besmirching the honour of my good friend, Sayo Mdang? The Private Secretary to the Lord Emperor?”

Apparently Lord Frugi was some weeks behind on the news, but not years behind. His eyes went wide as he identified Conju. Once Conju's words pierced his slowly moving brain, he flushed, and offered a bow of third-degree apology to Conju, ignoring Cliopher.

Conju saw red. Lord Frugi should have offered at least a second-degree apology to Cliopher. Ignoring him and offering only a third-degree apology to Conju was tantamount to declaring that Lord Frugi considered Cliopher to be an unimportant member of Conju's staff.

“Did you know, Lord Frugi,” said Conju, examining his perfectly manicured nails, “what happened in the days of the Empire, if the honour of a member of the Imperial household was insulted?” 

Conju looked up, his eyes cold, “No? The honour of any member of a household is always protected by the head, is it not? I see you still keep a pet duellist, so maybe you didn't know about the ban. If you are so insistent on insulting Sayo Mdang, however, I'm sure our lord could be persuaded.”

Lord Frugi looked from Conju to Cliopher, a slight sheen of sweat forming on his face. The manservant took two small steps backwards. Conju appreciated that at least one of the trio had some sense of self-preservation. Finally, Lord Frugi offered a much-belated second-degree apology to Cliopher.

Conju reached out to Cliopher, and rested a hand on his arm. If they *had* been back in Astandalas, that would have been the signal he used to tell his friend to accept the apology. Of course, Cliopher didn't know that, and looked at Conju in query. Conju made the court gesture for accepting an apology, with a quirked eyebrow at Cliopher. His friend got the hint, and mirrored him.

Cliopher looked back to Conju, with a slightly wry smile. “I do hope his Radiancy wouldn't lift the ban for _me_, Lord Conju,” he said smoothly. “He has been working so hard to ensure a more equitable justice system.”

“Yes, do tell me about the planned change in the censorship laws,” said Conju, slipping his hand into the crook of Cliopher's elbow, and turning them away from Lord Frugi. Cliopher positively beamed at Conju, and began a long explanation of the benefits of freedom of the press.
