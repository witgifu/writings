It was well after midnight, well into the morning hours, when Kip and Fitzroy finally thought to go to bed. Fitzroy seemed to have gone past tired in the last half hour, and into a kind of manic wakefulness that made Kip wonder when he had drunk so much coffee.

Conversely, Kip was struggling to keep his eyes open. Fitzroy brushed a hand along his shoulder. "Is is bed time?" he whispered.

Kip mumbled a vague ascent, and turned to use Fitzroy's arm as a pillow. He snuggled in, rubbing his cheek along Fitzroy's beautiful skin.

Fitzroy sighed, "I meant in a bed, beloved."

Kip ignored him. Fitzroy took a step towards the inn, and Kip was forced to stumble after to hold on to his pillow.

Slowly, Fitzroy lead Kip across the green, through the taproom, and up the stairs. Kip didn't make any complaints, even when he stumbled on the bottom step, or struggled to turn in the hall.

Finally, Fitzroy got Kip to the door of his room. "This is your room, Kip," he said softly, and started trying to pry Kip off his arm.

Kip made a small whimper of protest. 

"You can't sleep in the hallway," whispered Fitzroy, amused.

"No," mumbled Kip, into Fitzroy's shoulder.

Fitzroy tried again to pull Kip free. Kip clung even tighter. Fitzroy sighed, opened the door, and shuffled them in sideways.

They sat on the edge of the high Alinorial bed. Even with such proximity to real pillows, Kip seemed disinclined to trade Fitzroy for one of them.

Getting Kip's boots off with only one free hand was tricky. Kip offered no assistance, mumbling something about the Good Neighbours. Fitzroy despaired of removing any more of Kip's clothes.

"Do you want to lie down, beloved?" asked Fitzroy.

"Only," said Kip, rubbing his cheek again on Fitzroy's arm.

"Only what, beloved?"

"Stay," mumbled Kip, "neighbours can't take you."

Fitzroy sighed, and started wrestling his own boots off with one hand.
