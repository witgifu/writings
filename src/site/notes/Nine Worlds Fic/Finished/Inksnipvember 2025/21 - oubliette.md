the soft sweet warmth of westering winter sun
and cool caress of summer's mistress moon
a breath of autumn breeze, leaves one by one
these all are lost, mere memories too soon

this dreary damp that seeps and drips down stone
I lick with parchment tongue, and long to linger
as if with purest spring, and lustily moan
but nothing more is found by questing finger

yes once in other days I had a name
but it was also swallowed by this shackle
though crying clawing cringing here I came
a creature, a little more than chattel

in this furrow, to be forgotten and forget
here under the black bars of my oubliette
