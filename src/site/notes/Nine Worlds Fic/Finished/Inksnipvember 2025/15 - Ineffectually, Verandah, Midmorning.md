Your midmorning refreshments were tea, and some kind of biscuit. Every day, you offered some to Cliopher. He only took water. He thanked you, in that way that was far to informal, and which gave me such a thrill.

Conju dressed you for court, and shared gossip. One evening, he told of how one of the Jilkanese princes had been caught sneaking into a tea party to eat all the sweet rice balls.

Carefully, you said, "I wonder what the young prince ignored, in his quest. It might be nice to try more regional specialties."

Conju smiled, and bowed, and you saw he understood.

The next day, there were custard tarts. Cliopher declined. Wafers, little sandwiches, peanut crackers, tiny button sugar cookies with multi-coloured swirls of icing. A parade of delicacies, some of which you even quite enjoyed. None of which tempted Cliopher. (The game was not going well.)

A month in, there were steamed buns, shaped and coloured like peaches. Seeing them, you sighed internally, knowing they could not possibly contain fresh peach.

Cliopher declined. You nibbled on a bun, watching him write out a neat copy of a proclamation. The bun, it transpired, contained some kind of caramel-flavoured paste.

He brought the proclamation over for your seal, and you put down the bun. As he slid it into place on your desk, you watched his gaze flit to the partially-eaten bun. You saw a hitch of indrawn breath. He recognised these buns.

You decided to risk a comment. "These buns this morning are quite good."

"Indeed, my lord," said Cliopher, "I have not seen lotus buns before in the Palace, though there is a Jilkanese restaurant in Solaara that makes them."

You ate all four buns, even thought you found them a bit too sweet.

Three days later, the buns returned. You asked, "Tea, water, lotus bun, Sayo Mdang?" Out of the corner of your eye, you watched him hesitate. You carefully didn't look at him directly.

"Thank you, my lord, a lotus bun would be lovely," he said, and put down his brush.
