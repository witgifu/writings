If all the lights were dimmed and I alone
(Now I have learnt the length of every wall)
I'd break a breath before the bars of bones
The warning wail within the clearest call

I know their names, the rhythm rules my reflection 
Eyes closed, their cries will echo in my ears
In the pounding of my pulse I promise penetention
And vow (in vain?) to value the valour of their fears

(Were I, alone, allowed to weep and wail?)
I only shiver in the shadows of my mind
'Smile', so sing the ossuaries in minor scale
I try to train my thoughts, my body bind

I do not sing, or slouch, or sigh, but stand
And smile serene as they heat again the brand
