Bracing himself, Rhodin pulled Cliopher down the back stairs to the kitchen door of Conju's apartment. Negotiating getting Cliopher out of the Offices of State without anyone realising had been difficult. Rhodin had felt eyes around every corner, and it had been impossible to convince Cliopher to keep his thoughts inside that brilliant head.

“Where are we going?” whispered Cliopher, his breath tickling Rhodin's shoulder.

“Somewhere private,” answered Rhodin, silently begging to the stars above for Conju to be there. The Groom of the Chamber's schedule had his afternoon nap now. But Rhodin knew that he was often forced to cut it short, or start it late, if something happened. Usually that was with his Radiancy, not with Cliopher. He hoped their friend wouldn't be too angry at them turning up unexpectedly.

Cliopher clung even tighter to his arm, and said, as if this was the kind of thing he would typically ask, “Do you wear anything under your kilt? I've always wondered.” He followed this up by running a hand down Rhodin's back, leaving it just above the waistband of the kilt.

Guards needed to be able to be stoic and unresponsive in the most trying circumstances. Rhodin's skills were being severely tested. He was worried that Cliopher would be mortified, whenever he got back to normal. Which would surely be tonight, most aphrodisiacs metabolised quickly.

“Sometimes,” said Rhodin, trying to pretend he didn't know why Cliopher was asking. He moved on quickly, “What were you doing when Saya Kalikiri visited your office? Having a late lunch?”

“Yes,” said Cliopher, now running his fingertips along the ripples of Rhodin's arm muscles. “Thankfully, someone had left a delicious caramel slice on my desk. Where are we going again?”

Deciding prudence was more important than politeness, Rhodin opened the kitchen door without even knocking, and tugged Cliopher inside. Conju had added him to the magical key years ago, on the grounds that his staff found it less worrying than finding Rhodin using the windows. It hadn't, of course, _stopped_ him using the windows. But the door was much easier when trying to bring someone else. With it shut behind them, Rhodin felt able to relax infinitesimally.

Moments later, Conju himself appeared, seeming incongruous in his own kitchen. “Rhodin? What has happened?”

“Cliopher has consumed some adulterated baked goods,” said Rhodin, trying to peel the man in question from his elbow.

“Adulterated with _what_?” demanded Conju, looking sharply between the two of them, “And why on earth did you let him eat them?”

Rhodin looked appropriately sheepish. “I'm not entirely sure how,” he admitted, “I have started enquiries.”

“Conju!” said Cliopher brightly. He finally let go of Rhodin, only to clamp himself to the other man. “Did I ever tell you how absolutely stunning your eyelashes are?”

“Thank you, dear,” said Conju, looking a little strained, “You have mentioned before how much you appreciate his Radiancy's. I make the same serum.”

“Of course!” said Cliopher, “He's not here too, is he?”

Conju gave Rhodin a clear and wide-eyed look of dismay. Turning back to Cliopher, he said, “No, of course not, he is in his private study now.”

“Oh,” said Cliopher, looking slightly disappointed. Then he lent over to whisper in Conju's ear. “You're very nearly as beautiful, I think. And of course, I can't give him a hug like this.”

It took Conju a moment to squeeze out a small “no”, and then he managed to move Cliopher crab-wise further into the apartment. Rhodin followed, curious what Cliopher might say next.

“Do you recognise…” asked Rhodin, quietly, “It doesn't seem quite like ardmoor, wireweed, or any poppy extract I'm familiar with.”

“It's a cocktail,” whispered Conju, “It used to be… well, that would be illegal now. The best antidote is time, hydration, and sleep. If you can convince the victim to do anything so sensible.”

“I'm very sensible of how much care you take of yourself,” said Cliopher brightly, “Both of you.”

“Thank you, dear,” said Conju absently, pushing Cliopher into a spare bedroom. It was presumably where Cliopher stayed if he'd had a little too much to drink to walk back to his own rooms. Rhodin appreciated it had rather fewer delicate ornaments than most of Conju's rooms. In his current state, Cliopher was rather likely to knock them over with an enthusiastic gesture.

“Oh!” said Cliopher, looking around. Then he turned back to Conju and Rhodin with a look the guard didn't recognise at all. “When you said somewhere private, well...” he looked at the bed, and then licked his lips. Rhodin swallowed hard.

“You need to _rest_,” said Conju, gently trying to pry Cliopher's grip loose.

“Don't look like that, Conju,” said Cliopher, with a smile. He leaned in to nuzzle against Conju's neck.

Conju took a deep breath, and said, “No, no, Cliopher. You really _must_ lie down and rest. I know you are feeling on top of the world at the moment, but shortly you are going to have a frightful headache.”

Cliopher looked between them. “Unfair,” he said, and crossed his arms.

“If you are still enthusiastic in two bells, while his Radiancy is at Afternoon Court, we will discuss it then,” said Conju, firmly.

Cliopher looked muleish as he kicked off his sandals. “Fine,” he said, grumpy.

Conju walked over to the bedside, and poured him a glass of water from the waiting carafe. “His Radiancy would be furious if I didn't look after you,” he said passing over the glass.

That was perhaps not worded quite as wisely as it might have been. For the first time Rhodin had known him, Cliopher saw a possible double entendre. He smiled again, that strange smile that Rhodin still found worrying.

“Please,” said Cliopher, breathlessly.

“I… should continue my investigations,” said Rhodin, and edged his way out of the room. Perhaps there *was* more to Conju and Cliopher's friendship than he had seen before. Cliopher, when not drugged out of his mind, was always exceedingly discrete.
