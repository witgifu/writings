Cliopher corned Conju near the kitchen shortly after dinner.

“Can I ask you something?” said Cliopher, trying to pretend he wasn't blocking Conju.

Conju raised an eyebrow, “What about?” he countered.

“I know you have special spelled gloves,” said Cliopher, “Just… just in case.”

“Yes,” said Conju tersely, “I am very thankful that so far I haven't ever had cause to need them. Why? Is there something wrong?” He looked suddenly anxious, and peered over Cliopher's shoulder as if he could see the Sun Cabin.

“Nothing is wrong!” said Cliopher, quickly. “But I was wondering, does his Radiancy have any?”

Conju looked at Cliopher in confusion, “Whatever for?”

“I was hoping it might be possible for him to hold Aurenia,” said Cliopher, timidly, “I didn't want to mention it to him, in case it couldn't be done.”

They both looked at Aurenia. She was awake, but sleepy, and snuggled into Cliopher's chest in the baby carrier.

“Yes, it is possible,” said Conju, slowly, “Eritanyr — well, let's just say he tested the taboos. His Radiancy doesn't have any gloves though, nor any face coverings. He was, I believe, very insistent that none of Eritanyr's… experiments… were going to continue.”

“I know it will take time to make them, but could you? Please?” asked Cliopher.

“Very well, Cliopher. But *you* can present them, and if he is at all disturbed by them, I am burning them and blaming you, understood?” said Conju.

Cliopher could tell how worried Conju was about even suggesting it to their lord. “Yes, Conju,” he replied, seriously. Then he stood aside and allowed Conju to pass.

Three steps down the hall, Conju turned back to Cliopher. “I packed an emergency poncho,” he said, sounding like the words were being dragged from him unwillingly.

It was not quite clear to Cliopher how a rain covering could be relevant.

Conju clarified, “If there was a medical emergency. It wouldn't be possible to have coverings made to fit every medical practitioner in the Vangaveye-ve. It has mitts attached.” Seeing the excitement fawning on Cliopher's face, he held up a hand.

“You may have it *only* if you agree to find a way to make sure his Radiancy is *at least* able to sleep from midnight until dawn.” Conju dropped the hand, and gave Cliopher a hard look.

Cliopher thought seriously, before answering. “I will sleep for however long his Radiancy can hold the spell, or until midnight, whichever comes first. Or, if he and her are amenable, he could hold Aurenia. Regardless, from midnight to dawn, she and I will vacate the Sun Cabin.”

Conju rolled his eyes. “You don't have to make it a _contract_, Cliopher,” he said, making 'contract' sound like an infectious disease, “But I accept.”

Cliopher followed Conju through the Sun Cabin and into the dressing room. There the Groom of the Chamber pulled out a trunk clearly labelled 'medical'. After carefully lifting out a number of packages and placing them to the side, he handed one to Cliopher. Then he rapidly repacked everything, before shooing Cliopher out.

His Radiancy was sitting in an armchair, holding one of the parenting books, but looking out the window. If he had been reading, Cliopher might have chickened out, but this seemed like the perfect time.

“My—Tor?” he began, and his Radiancy turned back from the window.

“Yes, Kip?” he said, with unusual warmth.

Cliopher placed the package down on the small table next to his lord, much as me might have done with documents that needed his lord's seal.

Taking a deep breath, Cliopher tried not to give himself time to panic and back out. “I understand perfectly if you would rather wait, but I thought that would probably be many months or maybe years, so I wanted to give you the option, just in case, I know it isn't the same, and if you choose to use it or not I will still do everything I can as fast as possible to make it not needed, my—Tor.”

His Radiancy looked at Cliopher, and then the package, and then back at Cliopher. “You haven't said what it is, Kip,” he said, and Cliopher thought he detected a note of amusement.

“It's a protective garment,” said Cliopher, weakly, “So… so you could hold… Aurenia. If, if you wanted to.”

Aurenia could obviously feel Cliopher's rising distress. She made a soft mewing sound, not quite a cry, and wriggled in the carrier. Cliopher took the opportunity to look down at her, and kiss her on the top of her head. “And if you wanted, Aurenia,” he said softly.

When Cliopher finally looked up, his Radiancy had picked up the package, and was holding it in trembling hands. Cliopher couldn't read his lord's face, but his instincts were screaming at him to apologise and beg forgiveness. He tried to resist, to wait.

“Why is this here?” said his Radiancy, in the careful even tones. That Cliopher had so recently learnt were always hiding his strong emotions.

“Conju packed it, my lord, for medical emergencies. If, if something happened to you… and we had to call a local doctor. I am sorry, my lord, it was my idea to get it out, please do not blame him.” Unable to resist any longer, he sank to his knees.

His Radiancy finally looked from the packet, to Cliopher. “I'm not angry, Kip,” he said softly, stroking the package, “I'm just… surprised. Overwhelmed. Thank you, Kip. Please get up.”

Using a chair for leverage, Cliopher pulled himself up.

His Radiancy used a trembling finger to tear the paper seal on the package. The contents looked like a very fine, translucent, white silk. At least to Cliopher's completely non-magical sight. Pinching gently, his Radiancy pulled it from the envelope, and it flowed out.

Rather than a poncho, it seemed perhaps to be the world's largest and floppiest hat. With, as Conju had said, sleeves ending in mitts. There was a small opening for the eyes as well.

Finally sensing it was safe, Conju emerged from the dressing room. He bowed to his Radiancy, and said quietly, “May I assist you, my lord?”

His Radiancy ignored the offer, and pulled the silk over himself. He found the sleeves, and the mitts, both comically large on his slender frame. Then he used his covered hands to settle the crown of the garment on his head.

Cliopher stepped forward, and his Radiancy hastily arranged the bottom of the garment to cover his legs and knees. Stepping closer again, he lifted Aurenia out of the carrier. Slowly, carefully, he lowered her onto his Radiancy's silk-covered lap.

“Wrap one arm around her,” said Cliopher gently, “Yes, just like that.”

Cliopher half-expected Aurenia to cry immediately. She hadn't accepted being handed off to anyone else while on the sky ship. And surely the garment practically stank of schooled magic. But she looked up at his Radiancy's face and said, “Ooor.”

His Radiancy, even through the blur of the garment, was obviously charmed. He held out his other covered hand to Aurenia. She grabbed it with both hands and waved it up and down. “Oooor,” she said again.

Cliopher sank into a chair, immensely relieved. This was the happiest he'd seen Aurenia since they'd boarded the sky ship. Perhaps somehow his Radiancy's wild magic, when so close, overwhelmed the schooled magic. Or maybe the protective garment was helping. Cliopher didn't understand enough about magic to know. 

All he knew was that both his lord and his daughter seemed delighted.

“I will get you something better fitting as soon as we reach Solaara, my lord,” said Conju, obviously relieved.

“Thank you, Conju,” said his Radiancy, softly.

Aurenia, quite naturally, reached out and grabbed a fistful of the fabric covering his Radiancy. Both Cliopher and Conju lunged forward, while his Radiancy twitched backwards. They were all terrified that Aurenia could pull off the garment and endanger herself. 

Thankfully, there was sufficient fabric that no matter how she waved her hand, the crown did not even slide slightly on his Radiancy's head. “Eeeei,” said Aurenia, unperturbed by their behaviour.

All three men took a deep breath. 

“May I pin it to your robes, my lord?” said Conju, his voice unusually shaky, “Just, just to be sure.”

“Please,” said his Radiancy, weakly.

A container of golden pins appeared in Conju's hand, and he carefully secured the layers, making sure all pins were out of reach of Aurenia's hands.

“Can I get you a book or a toy, my—Tor?” ask Cliopher, wondering what to do next.

His Radiancy smiled at Aurenia, “Would you like that, little one?”

“Eeeei!” she replied, smiling up at his Radiancy.

Cliopher dug out a stuffed duck, that had so far been her favourite. Cautiously, he put it directly in his Radiancy's free, but mitt-covered, hand. His Radiancy looked at Cliopher with wonder in his eyes.

Watching his Radiancy make up a story about the duck, as he waved it around, Cliopher smiled. He was even more determined to see his lord free of the taboos. How could such a loving, expressive man, be trapped away from his daughter? As his Radiancy pretended the duck was eating Aurenia's toes, Cliopher had to look away. He wiped away a couple of sad, frustrated tears.

---

His Radiancy and Aurenia spent a happy half bell or so playing together. Then Aurenia started fussing.

“I can take her to the toilet and feed her,” said Cliopher, with a smile. “When I bring her back she will probably be ready for a nap.”

His Radiancy nodded, as Aurenia's fussing turned more upset. Kip picked her up, grabbed a bag of supplies, and headed for the bathroom.

When he returned, as predicted, he had a sleepy baby in his arms.

“Could I hold her again? While she naps?” asked his Radiancy, sounding a little uncertain.

“Please,” said Cliopher, “And if you don't mind, I will nap too. I'll trade you, and take her up on deck after midnight.”

His Radiancy nodded, arranging himself in a chair.

“Are you sure, Tor?” asked Cliopher, “Wouldn't the bed or the lounge be more comfortable?”

His Radiancy raised an eyebrow. “I sit perfectly straight for six-hour court sessions. At least here I can _lean back_.”

Conju helped his Radiancy arrange and pin the protective garment in place. When Cliopher handed Aurenia over, she made a small snuffle of protest about being moved, but then nestled into his Radiancy's arms. Cliopher put the baby carrier on the table next to them, with a look at Conju, who nodded.

“Please wake me, Conju, if I'm not up in time,” said Cliopher, as he pulled off his outer layers.

“Of course,” said Conju, with a strong undertone of _I was going to do that anyway._

Cliopher curled up in his bed. He fell asleep watching his Radiancy gently stroking Aurenia's back.
