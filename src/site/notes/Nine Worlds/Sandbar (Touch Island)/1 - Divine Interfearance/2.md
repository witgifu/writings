Some tension still remained, as an undercurrent over the next few days. Thankfully it wasn't long until the Lunar Eclipse ceremony.

His Radiancy clearly enjoyed playing for an audience very much. He finished with a sad love song to the moon, before quietly announcing, “It is nearly the eclipse.”

Starting with the smallest toddler, they all offered their prayers to the Moon in the fire. When it came to Cliopher's turn, he spoke a childhood prayer. Into the fire he tossed his cowrie, with a wish he could give his lord the happiness and freedom he so obviously wanted.

There was, perhaps, a deeper wish under that. But even in his mind, he could not fully confess his heart to the Moon.

Last came his Radiancy. “Once more I invoke the Moon, that she look with favour upon all those under her shadow tonight, that we who cast our voices with our shadows upon her glory may be heard by her. Hear our wishes and our dreams and our desires. Hear our hearts laid open to you. Hear me, O Moon Lady!” 

He cried the last, tossing his stone, his eyes blazing in the light, his face impassioned.

A white hand caught it. Stepping out of the fire, a woman paler than any human. Inhumanly beautiful, shining elegant. The Moon Lady. 

“I hear you,” she said, to his Radiancy.

They talked, the Moon Lady, and the Sun-on-Earth. She seemed unconcerned by their audience, his Radiancy fully aware. Cliopher watched in astonishment as the Moon kissed his lord's hands, his face, his neck. For a moment, Cliopher wondered if his Radiancy would take up the Moon's offer, if he would leave them for her country. What could they offer (what could he offer?) that the Moon could not?

His Radiancy kissed the moon back, the passion shared. There was a flash of blinding light. Then the Moon was gone, the eclipse over.

Cliopher stood for a moment, blinded by the light and his own thoughts. He looked around, everyone was talking, chattering. But for his Radiancy, and Ludvic.

His lord rose unsteady from the chair, and Cliopher dashed over to him. “My lord?” Cliopher said, as his Radiancy considered the descent from the platform. Cliopher, once again, offered his golden hand.

His lord took it, leaning gently on Cliopher as they descended from the platform. “Excuse us,” he said to Jiano and Aya, and then set off at a brisk pace. Still holding tight to Cliopher.

His Radiancy was luminous, as if the moonlight clung to him as pollen or smoke. No mage light was required for the path, and Cliopher wondered if this was how it had been when the Sun-on-Earth had glowed with the taboos. The silver light from his black skin was beautiful, glittering more spectacularly than any of the gems or gold ever did.

When Cliopher caught a glimpse of his lord's eyes, it was as if the shining gold was the only colour in the world. Cliopher almost stumbled, it took his breath away.

They broke from the trees onto the beach. Freed from the need to watch his steps so closely, his Radiancy sped up even further. He moved so fast, it was almost a run. Not wanting to pull away from his lord's hand, Cliopher sped up as well.

Ludvic ran ahead to open the doors.

Once they were inside, his Radiancy stopped and looked around the room, breathing hard.

Ludvic saluted. “In Sayo Mdang's room, my lord, the window louvres are fixed, and we could stand outside the door.”

Cliopher hadn't thought that far ahead. He had been admiring his lord, and yes, the image of the Moon's hand questing under his lord's robes was flickering in Cliopher's mind. If his lord wanted… The heady feeling in his chest even burned past his fears about his inexperience. He trusted his lord.

Also, now that Ludvic pointed it out, he realised how generous the compromise he offered was. Cliopher was not sure how he would have felt about the guards watching.

Conju nodded to Ludvic, bowed to his Radiancy, and bustled past them into the corridor.

“I … what? … I am sorry, I … am most discomposed,” said his Radiancy, looking at Ludvic.

Ludvic looked from his Radiancy, to his lord's right hand, still clasping Cliopher's left. His Radiancy followed Ludvic's gaze. Then up Cliopher's arm to his face. Their eyes met.

“I … you … ?” said his Radiancy, his eyes wide. It was the most emotional Cliopher had ever seen his lord. Want, confusion, fear, sorrow, seemed to be warring within him. 

Cliopher squeezed his lord's hand. “Yes,” he said quietly, looking deep into those eyes. 

He loved his lord, he would give him anything at all that he could. His lord had asked for peace, and he had found the thread from which it could be woven. His lord had asked for a solution to the slums in the large cities, and he had wrangled the council of princes into agreeing to it. Whenever his lord despaired of the situation in Nijan, he went to try to help them talk it through. He had asked the Moon to bring his lord happiness, but maybe that was something *he* could give. Or help him find.

Slowly, slowly, Cliopher lifted up his lord's hand. Keeping their eyes locked, he raised it to his lips, and brushed the smallest kiss on the back.

His Radiancy swallowed suddenly, with a shake through his whole body. “Cliopher … you can't ...” he said.

Cliopher tilted his head slightly, as he tried to parse his lord's meaning. Did he think he didn't mean it? That it was an obligation, like attending court ceremonies? That there was someone else in Cliopher's life?

“I love you,” he said.

His Radiancy took in a deep, shuddering breath.

“Are you well, my lord?” asked Ludvic.

“No … yes ...” his Radiancy answered, tearing his eyes away from Cliopher momentarily, to look bewildered at his guards.

Cliopher rubbed his finger gently across his lord's palm. He took a small step forward, so now he had to tilt his head up to look into his lord's face. “Tor?” he breathed, with a daring he hadn't known he had.

His Radiancy let out a small puff of breath, like a sigh. He lifted their entwined hands, and kissed the back of Cliopher's hand. A mirror of what Cliopher had done. “Kip,” he breathed back.

His mind fizzing with joy, Cliopher reached out his free hand, up towards his Radiancy's face. Gently, he cradled the soft, warm cheek. His Radiancy almost closed his eyes, and lent gently against Cliopher's hand.

“Tor,” said Cliopher again, this time in a whisper.

His Radiancy, Tor, bent his head down until his forehead touched Cliopher's. Their breath mingled. How many people had Cliopher greeted this way? And yet with his — Tor, it felt like lightning with every breath.

Cliopher could feel his pulse in his whole body. He lifted his heels off the floor, reaching up, so the tip of his nose touched his — Tor's. Still cradling Tor's cheek in his right hand, Cliopher brushed the tip of his nose down the side of Tor's, pushing it into the fold between cheek and nose.

“Kip,” breathed Tor, and with the final plosive, his lips brushed Cliopher's. They were so soft, Cliopher thought. And then he didn't think, his lips *wanted*, and he reached up that fraction farther to push his lips onto Tor's.

Cliopher felt Tor breathe in and hold, and almost pulled back. But then Tor pushed down, into the kiss. Tor's other hand came up Cliopher's back, resting on his shoulder muscles, keeping him close.

Cliopher didn't usually dream, or daydream, about kissing, but he thought perhaps he might make an exception for this kiss. It was… magnificent. Maybe it was only the effect of the Moon on them both, but it seemed the whole world had narrowed to this sensation, this feeling shared.

He lent in to Tor's body, chest to chest, thigh to thigh. It felt so right, so good, so safe, here with Tor. Thinking possibly to breathe or move, Cliopher broke the kiss, but felt so immediately bereft that he had to go back.

Sliding his hand down to Tor's neck, Kip remembered they had an audience. Were in the common room, with at least two guards, and possibly Conju. And Ludvic had offered them somewhere more private.

Slowly, gently, he moved his lips away from Tor's. “Tor,” whispered Cliopher, still close enough he could feel Tor's breath on his lips, “Please, come with me?”

Tor's eyes were closed, and he seemed lost in the moment. He licked his lips, and his tongue brushed Cliopher's lips as well. “I don't want...” he whispered back, and Cliopher tried to resist a rising panic. Had he not wanted the kiss? Was this some spell placed by the moon that he was trying to fight?

One small tear escaped his Radiancy's closed eyes. Cliopher couldn't help brushing it away with his thumb. He wanted to say he would give his lord anything at all, but if felt like the time to wait, and listen. So he held his silence, as he held his lord's face and hand.

“I don't want to be an obligation,” whispered his lord eventually.

All that his Radiancy, Tor, had said these past days crashed back into Cliopher's mind. That he was a man, with emotions that ran fast and strong. But that he had to contain them, put himself into a small box, to keep everyone else safe.

“I love you, Tor,” whispered Cliopher, “Love freely given.”

Tor took a great shuddering breath. Cliopher could feel it vibrating through his chest as well. But Tor didn't explain, or pull away, he just held on to Cliopher as if he was worried Cliopher might vanish. 

So Cliopher said, “Tor?”

Tor's eyes opened, and he looked down into Cliopher's. So close, Cliopher could see they had tiny flecks of darker and brighter gold in them. Like Imperial Yellow cloth, made of many colours.

Still, Tor said nothing more. Cliopher tried again, “Tor, what are you afraid of?”

Tor kissed Cliopher, quickly. As if he was trying to ignore the question, although Cliopher wasn't sure which of them he was trying to distract. He lent into the kiss anyway.

Cliopher didn't want to ask a third time. It would fit with a requirement to answer, in a tale or a challenge song. He didn't want to *force* Tor to answer, if he didn't want to. Tor had a right to keep his feelings to himself if he wished.

Eventually Tor broke the kiss. Then he placed a small kiss on the top of Cliopher's nose. “Kip,” he whispered, “I'm afraid of hurting you.”

Cliopher wanted to say that he wouldn't, that Cliopher trusted him completely. “I will tell you what I like, and what I don't, and if I feel uncomfortable. Will you do the same?”

He gently brushed his thumb on Tor's jawline. Trying to ground him, keep him here, and away from the frightening places his imagination was taking him. Cliopher kissed him on the top of his nose, the corners of his mouth, his chin.

Tor seemed lost. Cliopher could feel his body still hungered, still pressed together, Tor still holding Cliopher close. But his mind had wandered, trapped in some other time or place.

The easy choice, the sensible choice, would be to step away. To go back to small, careful touches. But Cliopher wanted to see more of the beautiful man under the golden shell. The care, the laughter, the joy, that the box had not had room for. And yes, even the anger and the tears, if needed. Cliopher wanted to set him free, he wanted to throw open every door and window.

It would be seven years to the Jubilee, seven years of obligations his lord could not avoid. Cliopher would do everything he could to make that easier. If this was one way to do so, he needed to keep hold of it. Keep hold of Tor.

“Tor,” said Cliopher softly, “I don't want to hurt *you*.”

Tor pulled him tight for a moment. “You won't, Kip,” he said, softly.

Cliopher squeezed Tor's hand in his. “Because you'll tell me before then, yes? Not because you'll redefine 'hurt' because you're afraid?”

Closing his eyes, Tor stretched his neck, leaning into Cliopher's hand. He sighed, and gave the tiniest nod. Then he lent down for another kiss.

Now Cliopher relaxed into the kiss, letting Tor lead it. The spark and fizz of the first kiss had turned into something burning steady between them. Fed with words and touches, Cliopher could feel it in every point where skin touched skin.

He pulled back slightly, and gave Tor a languid smile. “Shall we… ?” he asked, and flicked his eyes to the door into the hallway.

Tor seemed to have run out of words. He nodded again, biting his lip. Cliopher slowly drew his hand down Tor's neck and chest, lifting it away a finger at a time. He twisted, and kissed the inside of Tor's elbow.

“Ahhhh...” said Tor, his arm falling limply down.

Cliopher walked briskly to the door, which Ludvic opened with a small smile. Cliopher pulled Tor along behind him, past the door to his Radiancy's bedroom, and down to his own. Taking a deep breath, he opened the door and pulled Tor in. Then he turned back, and shut it behind them.

Tor's eyes opened wide at the sound, and his mouth made a small 'o' of surprise. Cliopher tilted his head to the side in question. “Do you—” he started, but Tor interrupted.

“No. I just never thought… oh, Kip...” he said. Tor closed the gap between them again, and untangling their hands, he brought both up to Cliopher's face, cupping it between them.

Cliopher shut his eyes, relishing Tor's wonderfully soft hands. He rolled his head gently, and brought his own hands up to Tor's waist. Tor ran a line of kisses from the top of Cliopher's head, down to his temple, around Tor's fingers. Ending at Kip's mouth.

Taking small steps, and tugging gently on Tor, Kip turned his back to the bed. Then he pulled Tor towards it, until the side of the bed hit the back of his calves. He slipped his hands around to Tor's back, and then flopped backwards onto the bed. As planned, Tor fell on top of him.

“Oh!” said Tor, and then laughed. It was music to Cliopher's ears. Sparkling, bright, unfettered by expectations. Tor rolled slightly to the side, so not all of his weight was on Cliopher.

Unable to resist closing the distance, Cliopher reached up his golden palm, and rubbed it over the back of Tor's head. Tor fluttered his eyes closed, and let out a small sigh. Cliopher pulled Tor's head down, and lifted his own slightly, for another kiss.

It became too much for Cliopher to hold his head up, and he dropped it back onto the bed. Tor chased Cliopher's lips down, and peppered him with small pecks. Then he spread out, onto Cliopher's cheeks and nose and chin. Finally, he drew a line of kisses from Cliopher's chin, down his neck, to the collar of his robes.

Tor paused, looking at Cliopher's collar. Cliopher didn't want Tor to stop, and pulled his head down towards Cliopher. There was a glint in Tor's eye, as he collapsed onto Cliopher again.
