As you entered your dressing room, you asked if someone could bring the book of poetry for you to read that night. You stepped up, and stood on the plinth. The marble base on which they painted and un-painted the idol of their Emperor.

The fear of touching someone accidentally again had dulled over the years, but the chances were highest when they were so close. You closed your eyes, and focused all your thoughts on staying absolutely and perfectly still. Counting your breaths, ignoring any sound, any feeling on your body.

It was never an exercise conductive to sleep.

When you entered your bedroom, Sayo Mdang was there. For a moment you had a terrified jolt that the nightmare had happened, and they would force him...

But no. He was dressed in his uniform. He bowed low to you, a book in his hands. *The* book, you noticed. He placed it on your nightstand, and stood back, waiting to be dismissed.

"Is this your book, Sayo Mdang?" you asked, suppressing how tired you felt. How much you did not want to be dealing with this problem. How scared you had just been.

He bowed again. "It was, Glorious One. But I know every word by heart, so if it please your Radiancy, I would be most glad for you to have it."

There could be nothing that you could see and desire that could be denied to you. No fine jewel, no masterful artwork, no mortal body. No book, however treasured by the current owner. You made the gesture to accept a tithe, my heart once again aching.

You dismissed them all, and got into bed. You left the book on the nightstand. You *had* been looking forward to reading it, but that had been tainted now. You had stolen the book. You, who had a copy of every book published in the Empire, more than any library or bookstore. You had stolen from a man who surely could have only brought a few books with him on the long journey across the Wide Seas and then to another world.

You were a monster. You cried, silently. Your tears sparkled in your own glow.
