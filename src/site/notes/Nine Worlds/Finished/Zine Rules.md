# Tales by the Fire Zine 01

## Submissions
We'll be collecting from the **8th of July to the 31st**, and hoping to release the finished pdfs of the zine in early August. If you want to have your work included, make sure to say "zine 01" and we'll be searching for that. Please remember to post crimes/nsfw in the appropriate channels, we'll check those too. You can submit as many things as you want! 
## Prompts
From the **1st to 7th of July** is 🔥 _Spark the Fire_ 🔥. We'll be sharing some prompts and themes, but this week is your chance to throw out ideas for others to pick up. If you want to colab with someone else, this is also time to set that up. Feel free to start on your ideas, but 🤫 no sharing until the **8th**. 

If you want to contribute something that doesn't fit a theme or prompt, no worries, we're happy to take that too. These are to help spark ideas, not limit you.
## Writing
1-1k words in any format. Fanfic, poetry, essay, etc. This should be easy to submit! You can post directly in discord (with spoiler bars) and we can copy it out. If you want _advanced formatting_ feel free to link google docs, canva designs, or other things we can easily copy your text from.
## Art
Photos, drawings, paintings, collage, printmaking, quilting, embroidery, crochet, etc, etc. Basically, anything _printable_, so not video or audio. (I guess... sheet music is fine?).

Formats:
* Digital art: PNG or SVG
* Traditional arts, photography: JPEG, PNG, or HEIC
Any resolution is fine, but the higher resolution the bigger we can make it on the page ;) 

Discord crunches pictures, and if you don't have Nitro it crunches them even more. So, while we'd love for you to post it directly it would be easier for compiling the zine if you could give us some way to get a high res version. You can add a link to your drive/box of choice, or upload to [this google drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1gLdUQtpXImBO1wU2d1bvhxw-lcIPOilN?usp=sharing) which belongs to @Witgifu (we promise to delete everything from there once the zine is compiled).
## Full pages
If you'd like to contribute an entire page... yay! We have a few different ways of collecting them:
1. Photo, see advice under "art" above, noting that at 300dpi, a full a4/letter page is ~2500 x 3500 px. Feel free to go larger, but try to get at least that big.
2. Share a Canva design
3. PDF (if your software has an option to 'flatten' the PDF, turn it off plz)
## Layout Squad
If you want to join in and help lay the pages out, watch out for a post from Mari during _Spark the Fire_ week. You'll need a Canva account, and enthusiasm. 
## Embargo
Please don't make your work public (ao3, tumblr, instagram, etc) until the final zine is posted for everyone :slight_smile: of course feel free to send a pic to your family groupchat or ask anyone for a beta.
