It was unusual for Conju to request his presence with a verbal message sent by one of the guards, and Rhodin couldn't help but worry. In fact it had never happened before, which either meant something was very wrong, or the message hadn't actually come from Conju. He had warily told Commander Omo exactly what had happened, just in case.

In case of *what*, he couldn't quite decide. Someone who wanted to lure him from the barracks would still have a full guard rota to get past. If instead they were hoping to get him alone, well they had reckoned without his obsession with alternative routes. Anyway, taking the same path twice was boring. He took a garden path in entirely the wrong direction, so he could enter the Palace by a side-door that was next to a concealed staircase.

The kitchen hand who opened the back door of Conju's apartment only nodded to him. Conju's staff were used to his antics. He passed through the service rooms, and towards Conju's bedroom. There he found a note wedged into the doorframe. It simply said 'Do not enter,' in Conju's elegant hand.

Taking the note at face value, Rhodin went do Conju's favourite parlour instead. Perhaps he had organised some kind of surprise? It seemed very unlike him. The Groom of the Chamber generally considered 'surprises', except those carefully planned and agreed upon by all parties involved, to be akin to sneezing on someone.

Conju was at his work table, mixing something. Rhodin knocked gently on the door frame.

"You're finally here," said Conju, not looking up from where he was very carefully measuring something liquid and silvery that Rhodin desperately hoped wasn't mercury. "I had thought asking for you through the official channels might have brought you sooner, but apparently not."

"My apologies, the message did not say it was urgent. Is something wrong?" It honestly hadn't occurred to Rhodin that Conju might be requesting the Deputy-Commander, rather than a friend.

"Where did you last sleep?" enquired Conju, not giving anything away about why he might need that information.

It was a test. Curious, Rhodin replied honestly, "I took a nap in the Emperor's shoe cupboard after my morning shift. Lady Ylette woke me at the fourth hour."

Conju nodded, and slipped something from his hand to his pocket. Not a knife, Rhodin was sure. A vial.

"Come and see what I caught," said Conju, leading the way back to his bedroom, "And then you can work out how to take it away."

When Conju opened the door it was a scene from one of Rhodin's favourite fantasies. Blindfolded, tied to all four bedposts so he was spread out face down. Some liquid glistening on his buttocks and thighs. Except that he was clearly unconscious. Also, Rhodin was standing in the doorway, fully clothed, and it was someone else lying on the bed, wearing his face.

"Did you knock him out with a spell or...?" asked Rhodin. He'd need the antidote to question the imposter. "And when did you suspect?" It was mildly disappointing that the stranger had got this treatment.

"Alcohol," said Conju, "Administered rectally. I knew it wasn't you after half a minute, when he asked where Cliopher was."

That was fair, it wasn't the kind of question Rhodin would ask Conju. He had much better ways of keeping tabs on the secretary with no sense of self-preservation. It was more likely Conju would ask Rhodin.

Knocking the man out with alcohol made the paperwork easy.

"Thank you for your consideration," said Rhodin, with a small bow, "And I can see why you would not want to let anyone else arrest them."

"The rumours are going to be bad enough as it is," said Conju, "I don't suppose I can convince you to take him out the back door?"

"Of course," said Rhodin, "I would not want to tarnish your reputation. How much alcohol?"

"A gō of 80 percent," said Conju with a sniff, "Just a sterilising solution, I wasn't going to waste my extraction solvents on him."

Rhodin did the calculation quickly in his head. About a dozen glasses of wine, surely enough to significantly impair anyone, especially if it was dosed quickly. He moved confidently, replacing Conju's restraints with the magical ones the senior guards had. It would probably be safest to put him in one of the cells set aside for magical prisoners. Rhodin couldn't sense any particular aura of magical gift, but he suspected whoever had given this spell out wouldn't want the person under it recognised or talking. It definitely had a whiff of dark magics, and that wasn't just the alcohol fumes.

There was no chance he was going to make it back to Conju tonight. Well, if the Cavalier would even want him. Having seduced and laid low an imposter, Conju had every right to a quiet and relaxing night.

Rhodin would be spending it getting the man secured, writing reports, and finding out how some of his hair or nails or semen got into enemy hands. Possibly one of his regular women had been blackmailed. Most inconvenient.

"Did he give you any trouble?" asked Rhodin, suddenly remembering that the Groom of the Chamber had been taken advantage of by someone who was physically larger and undoubtedly up to something nefarious.

Conju snorted. "Less than you," he said dismissively, "He clearly relied too much on magic, and not enough on learning about who he was impersonating."

"As always, Lord Conju, you have done excellent work," said Rhodin, "I'll get a colleague or two, and we will remove this nuisance from your rooms."

"Much appreciated," said Conju, "I would like to go to bed soon."

Rhodin bowed, and left the strange man drooling into the sheets. He wanted to kiss the top of Conju's head as he passed, but didn't dare. Another time, he thought, ruefully, as he made his way to Conju's front door. For now, he had work to do.
