Cliopher wakes, to see the stars above him. They are unfamiliar, and slightly blurry. He blinks a few times, but they don't seem to want to come into focus. And yet he is sure they are wrong. Well, wrong for Zunidh. 

He was on Zunidh, surely? He doesn't remember being on Alinor or Ysthar recently. And he used to know the stars of Ysthar. Well, perhaps they have changed in the ten thousand years, or however long it has been, since the Fall there.

Fall? Fall! He was falling before, that's the last thing he remembers. Heart pounding, he tries to sit up.

“Please do not move, sir,” says a voice to his left, young, and earnest. 

Perhaps it is one of the guard then. “Could you tell me what happened? The last thing I remember is looking over the edge of the roof,” says Cliopher, admittedly feeling a little dazed.

“You fell out of the sky, sir. Thankfully you managed to slow yourself, I assume you are a mage of some power? And then you dropped onto the grass beside the path,” says the voice.

Cliopher rolls onto his left side, as the young man, he can now just see in the pre-dawn light, startles, and squeaks. 

“Please do not move, sir! If you have broken something it could be made much worse!” he says, looking around frantically. He is wearing breeches, stockings, and a loose flowing shirt. Not a guard then.

“I feel fine,” says Cliopher, in his usual warm and brisk tone, “I'm afraid I haven't a drop of magic though. Would you mind telling me where I am?”

“Near the bank of the Rag, not far from Dart Hall,” says the young man, “But sir, please stay here and let me go for help.”

Cliopher recognises neither the river nor the hall. Well, at least the two of them are speaking Shaian without trouble, that presumably means he's within the bounds of the old Empire. 

But if he doesn't recognise the stars, then he's certainly not close enough to the Palace to make his first meeting of the day. Drat. He sits up, and the young man gasps again.

“I feel quite fine, Sayo…?” he says, stretching his neck. Yes, if the young man hadn't said he had fallen from the sky, he could be mistaken for thinking he had simply taken a nap on the grass. Which would be almost, but not quite, as astonishing.

“Mr. Jemis Greenwing, sir,” says the young man. Unfortunately, the name means nothing to Cliopher. “And you are, sir?”

“Cliopher sayo Mdang,” he says, racking his brain to try to remember where in the nine worlds uses 'mister' as a title. Alinor and Ysthar, he thinks, nowhere on Zunidh that he is aware of. But the chance of falling out of the sky next to someone on Ysthar who would address him in Shaian seems vanishingly remote.

“What duchy are we in, Mr. Greenwing?” he guesses.

“Fiellan,” says Mr. Greenwing, the anxiety in his voice rising. “Perhaps you have hit your head, Sayo Mdang? Please do let me go and fetch help. I'm a good runner, and it's not far.”

“Fiellan!” exclaims Cliopher, all thoughts of his morning meetings pushed from his mind, “Are we anywhere near St-Noire?”

“Not very far,” says Mr. Greenwing, now confused, “But Dart Hall is really just over the hill, let me go there first.”

In defiance of Mr. Greenwing's protests, Cliopher levers himself up. “If you have business at Dart Hall, Mr. Greenwing, by all means let's go there first. But if the Bee at the Border is still there, I would appreciate it if you could give me directions.”

Mr. Greenwing looks increasingly pained. “At least come and have some breakfast, Sayo Mdang. I'm sure Mr. Dart will be happy to meet you.”

“Of course, Mr. Greenwing, thank you,” says Cliopher, wondering at the formality of addressing ones spouse as 'mister'. “And would there happen to be a post office nearby? I should probably write to my lord and let him know where I am and that it will take me a while to get back.”

“There's a post office in Ragnor Bella, we can visit on the way. Where are you from, Sayo Mdang, if you don't mind me asking?” 

Mr. Greenwing seems very polite, and not at all put out by the brisk walk up the rise. Cliopher appreciates someone with a good walking pace.

“The Vangaveye-ve, on Zunidh,” Cliopher answers, “Are you a local to these parts?”

“Oh yes, born and raised,” says Mr. Greenwing. At the top of the rise they can indeed see below them a South Fillianese gentry home. “Dart Hall,” says Mr. Greenwing, “Home of my good friend, Mr. Dart, and his brother, the squire.”

Silently, Cliopher chides himself for assuming Mr. Greenwing and Mr. Dart were married. If he has always dreamed of a very good friend, why shouldn't someone else want the same. “I haven't been in Fiellan for many, many years,” says Cliopher, “Has anything interesting happened?”

“Perhaps!” says Mr. Greenwing, cryptically, “When were you here last?”

“Ah,” says Cliopher, “That would be the thirteenth year of Emperor Artorin?”

“Quite a bit then! I must admit, I was too young for most of it, and then I went away to university. But let me start with Ragnor Bella…”
