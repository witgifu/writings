Conju's assistant wakes him a bell before dawn, and he lets her know that it will be an all-staff handover today. He washes, and dresses in the clean clothes that had been sent up by his own household. Cosmetics can hide a night of little sleep, self-control can hide strong emotions. He cannot hide that Cliopher is missing, and they do not know when he will be back. Or if. He tries not to think what that might mean.

The staff hall behind the kitchen is packed. Wardrobe staff, kitchen staff, errand runners, everyone. They all turn when Conju enters, expectant. Everyone knows a last-minute all-staff meeting isn't going to be good news; all the night-shift have already heard about the schedule reduction.

“Good morning,” says Conju, looking around for the key faces of the senior staff, “Some of you are already aware that we will be operating a reduced social schedule. I have called you all here to clarify what that means, and why. 

“Sayo Mdang went missing last night. There was no foul play, but an accident has left him stranded on another world. His Radiancy, the Ambassadors, the Offices of State, and the Guard, will all be working towards his swift return. However, this will require a shift in our usual operations and his Radiancy's schedule.

“Firstly, his Radiancy will be making no court appearances.”

As Conju expects, a murmur goes around the room at this announcement. This hasn't happened since his Radiancy awoke after the fall. He knows it will make some people anxious, particularly anyone who was here for the dark days immediately after the Fall.

“The Grand Duchess will be hosting a reduced court schedule, starting two days from today. We will be cancelling every non-core ritual, every social dinner. Anything that is not essential to the government functioning.”

Even more startled looks. Conju understands, the household runs like a well-oiled machine because everything has been perfected over years of repetition. Change is hard.

“This _is not_ permanent. These changes are until Sayo Mdang is returned to us, or the end of the session, whichever is sooner. The changes are in place to give the government and his Radiancy the space they need to adapt. I will keep the senior staff informed of what will happen after the end of session as plans are made. 

“Please take advantage of this opportunity. If there is a storage cupboard you have never had the time to clean out, or a popular accessory that could use some repairs, this is your chance. Update your inventory, document your processes.” He pauses, and gives them a moment to think through what he has said.

“Does anyone have any questions?” asks Conju, with another careful check of the senior staff. The head chef, he knows, will be thrilled with the chance to do a deep clean of the pantries. Lady Ylette is already giving significant looks to some of her staff, who have presumably asked for time to do tasks and been refused.

A kitchen-hand finally asks, “My lord, if we have leave booked, is that cancelled?”

“No,” says Conju firmly, “A reduced schedule should actually make it easier for anyone to take leave.”

“Should we expect more events with the Ambassadors, my lord?” asks someone hiding behind the head chef.

“I would not expect more social events. There may be some working events, but the Ambassadors are coordinating primarily with the Guard and the Offices of State,” says Conju.

And so it goes on. After a quarter bell, and a few repeated questions, Conju closes the meeting with a reminder that the dressing staff are due at their posts shortly. Everyone nods and disperses. Lady Ylette gives Conju a sympathetic smile as she walks past. She knows that Conju and Cliopher are friends.

Conju arrives at the dressing room to give everything a quick check, before heading into the bedroom. The priests are in place for the dawn ritual. Looking at them, Conju wonders what would happen if the dawn ritual was not performed. His lord has managed to expel the observers, the choir. Could he not at least also expel the priests?

Should he stay back, to grill them about what 'necessary' means, or should he stay with his Radiancy? As Conju pulls back the sumptuous bed curtain, he looks to his Radiancy. This is a key moment in his work, to assess his lord's mood and likely inclinations for the day. 

In any other man, Conju would describe the appearance as harrowed. Did his lord sleep at all? He was not wandering the apartments, or the attendants or guards would have woken Conju. The bookmark in his book hasn't moved, and his fingers are still twisting in the lap blanket from last night. His Radiancy doesn't look at Conju, but his face changes to his usual serenity.

Taking a breath, and steeling himself, Conju closes the bed curtains. He briefly catches a flash of emotion on his lord's face. Was it surprise, or relief? Conju isn't sure. Determined now, Conju steps away from the bed.

The priests do not rank high enough to warrant masks, so *their* surprise is clearly visible to Conju. How many mornings have been exactly the same before this? And now Conju, not his Radiancy, has changed things. He walks over to them.

“His Radiancy is unwell. Please do what you can without him, he needs to rest. I will send for his doctor,” says Conju, taking control of the situation. He is calm, polite, insistent.

A speaking glances passes between the priests, and Conju suspects the junior one is asking if they should complain to their superiors. It is true, it is not Conju's place to make this demand of them. But they have to balance their desire to control their god with their own declaration that he is in control. Both priests nod in unison, and depart.

Conju does, in fact, ask one of the attendants to send for the Domina. He is clear that it is a non-urgent request. There is likely little immediate help she can offer, when his Radiancy is suffering a perfectly normal and proportional reaction to unexpected loss. However, Conju hopes she might have some suggestions for ways the household might be able to offer alternative foods to his Radiancy. Whenever he is particularly anxious or stressed, he struggles to eat.

Returning to the bedside, Conju bows low to the curtains. “My lord, the priests have agreed that they can do without, today. Would you like to rest more, my lord?”

Conju listens carefully for any response. He hears a slow, shaky, exhale. Oh no, has he gone too far? Was it too presumptive to send away the priests? He doesn't think his lord _enjoyed_ the ritual, but perhaps it provided a distraction? Or perhaps it was simply an anchor into the normalcy of the day, and now Conju has reminded his lord that Cliopher is missing.

His Radiancy makes the smallest cough. Startled out of his anxiety, Conju slips the curtain open less than a hand-width, to check on him. There is a tear track on his Radiancy's face (why do tears roll so easily into ears?), and his breath is shallow and irregular.

Instinctively, Conju steps back, and starts to fold down. His Radiancy is crying, and Conju is the cause, and he has failed, so utterly there cannot possibly be any forgiveness, but perhaps he can dare to hope that there can be mercy.

“Thank you,” whispers his Radiancy.

Conju wobbles, uncharacteristically unsteady, before he manages to reverse course and stand again. As if he has only made an unpracticed bow. His Radiancy is pleased, only overwhelmed. Conju did the right thing, and relief rises in him like a warm bath.

Looking again to his lord's face, Conju sees he is trying to compose himself. Well, Conju can at least give him a semblance of privacy. Dropping the curtain shut, Conju takes a more confident step back. 

Should he dismiss the dressing attendants? It will be slower if he is alone. Will his Radiancy be anxious to get to breakfast and the morning reports? To learn if anything has happened in the night. Or are an extra few moments of calm more appropriate? It is Conju's job not to need to ask, but they are all adrift today.

Conju walks back behind the screen where the morning bedroom attendant looks nervous. The priests are not the only ones disturbed by the change. Conju offers a nod and a smile. The young man takes a deep breath before returning the smile with a small bow.

Whatever Conju chooses, he needs to exude confidence, for the benefit of the staff. Stepping into the dressing room, Conju offers a smile there too.

“His Radiancy is a little fatigued,” he begins, and everyone is alert for the second half of the instructions, “following a magical working and a disturbed night.”

Nobody seems surprised, although there are a few winces of sympathy. Conju is pleased, he has never invoked both protocols before, but it seems to have been expected following his announcement at handover. A pair of diamond drop earrings are swapped for small gold hoops. The rest has been anticipated, and the quiet competence of the staff reassures him.

Conju returns to the bedroom to wait. His Radiancy has never shown any interest in eating in bed, but perhaps tea might be appreciated? Even if he is feeling too unwell to eat anything at breakfast, he will usually drink a cup of tea. Perhaps Conju can sneak a little honey into it. He sends one of the attendants to the kitchen with the request.

The tea tray is brought to Conju in good time. He takes it deftly into the bedroom, gently placing it to the side of the bed. The hot water has yet to be poured into the teapot, and the water pitcher is insulated to keep the water at the perfect temperature for green tea. Conju allows himself to make the tiniest sound as he adjusts the teacup.

There is an answering stir from the bed, as the quarter bell rings. His Radiancy is awake. Pouring the hot water over the tea leaves releases the fresh aroma. A little peach in this blend, giving Conju justification to sneak in a half-spoon of sugar to the cup. He ignores the little sand timer, and judges the brew time by the scent. He carefully does not wonder what Rhodin and Ludvic are doing, or if the Ambassador from Alinor has been able to provide any useful information. 

Suddenly, the curtain is pulled back, and his Radiancy emerges. Caught by surprise, Conju does not reach the slippers in time, and his Radiancy strides across to the middle of the room with _bare feet_.

“I've had an idea,” he announces to the guards. They salute. “Do either of you know if Cliopher had his writing kit on him?”

The guards look at each other briefly. “No, my lord,” says Oginu, “We will send someone to find out immediately.” And he passes word through the door.

“My lord?” asks Conju, half-way between the slippers and the tea service, “Would you like tea while you wait for the answer, or would you rather dress?”

His Radiancy turns, and Conju automatically lowers his eyes. When his lord doesn't answer for a few beats, Conju's heart rate increases again. What else can he offer, if those two options aren't suitable?  Tentatively, he offers the option he had dismissed earlier, “Or would my lord prefer breakfast in bed today?”

“We should dress,” says his Radiancy, and moves in the direction of the dressing room.

Conju does not *scramble* to follow, which would be undignified, but he does move briskly. He gestures to the attendant to remove the tea things as they pass. There will be a fresh set at breakfast.

While dressing, Conju gives his lord a brief reminder of the schedule for the day. “Saya Kalikiri will be here at the third bell, my lord,” he says, as he wipes his lord's head efficiently with lotion, “You have no guests planned for lunch, but then the Council of Princes in the afternoon. There is nobody invited to dinner, and evening court has been cancelled due to the long Council session.”

As always, his Radiancy is beautifully still. Conju has done a much reduced skincare routine. His lord's beautiful skin tone means that hiding a lack of sleep is more about poise than cosmetics. His Radiancy is, always, aware and in control. It is something Conju has always admired about him. So Conju only places a few careful strokes of shimmering gold, to provide a distraction.

At breakfast, Conju gives the stack of reports a withering look. They are always slightly higher whenever Cliopher's ruthless hand isn't available to thin them. Just as his Radiancy pulls the summary closer to read it, an attendant passes Conju a note.

As Conju flips the note open, he can see out of the corner of his eye that his Radiancy is frozen, right hand hovering over the summary. The note has one word, “Yes”, with Ludvic's initials at the bottom. It is tiny, clearly torn from a larger sheet. Conju resists the urge to find a pair of scissors and trim the edges. Smoothing the crease in the paper with his finger, Conju slips the note on top of the report summary.

(He has thread scissors in his pocket of course, but what if they were blunt when he needed to trim a thread off his Radiancy's outfit?)

His Radiancy traces the letters with the tip of his index finger. He looks at the stack of reports, the cup of tea, and back at the note. Conju suspects he is considering abandoning breakfast to work on his idea.

“My lord, if you can tell me what or whom you might need, I can have the workroom set up for you,” offers Conju, trying to convince him to stay, “There may be time before lunch, and you have the entire evening.”

The hand tracing the letters stops. “Whatever kit the guard would usually take for travelling in the wilderness. A compass, a selection of small pocketable and unremarkable items like buttons or copper coins. In the evening I'll need Rhodin and whoever Ludvic would like to send with him.” His Radiancy slides the note to the side, and picks up piece of flatbread with his other hand.

Conju bows, and starts trying to think of things he can find to fulfil 'unremarkable' pocketable items. Most things in the Tower are, of course, remarkable. And will his Radiancy need to touch them? Many of the items would not be purified, and some things like a penknife, simply impossible. He will collect them in categories, making sure to get both purified and unpurified in case it is important for the magic. The guards will pass on word to Ludvic.

His Radiancy eats slightly less than half the breakfast set before him. Whatever the idea he has had is, it has clearly invigorated him. Conju decides he will not worry about what his Radiancy could want with a wilderness survival kit.

After his Radiancy is safely ensconced in the study, Conju crams down a breakfast wrap that he hardly tastes. It is offensive to the work the chef put in, which he regrets. Sometimes, his days are like this. Thankfully not very often.

He visits Lady Ylette to request the specifically mentioned buttons, and to elicit further ideas. Some of her assistants have excellent ideas, like religious pendants or worry stones, keys, and stubs of pencil. They are also able to quickly produce a variety of handkerchiefs in very many fabrics.

The errand runners are having their most interesting day in years. Conju sends to the treasury for shells and base metal rings, the kitchen stores for walnuts, the gardens for pebbles, and the menagerie to ask if any of the animals have shed suitable teeth.

In two bells Conju has collected baskets full of items. He carefully labels them, purified, unpurified, purification not required, dangerous. All the staff know what to do if they have any further ideas.

Finally, feeling the strain of the night and the day, Conju decides he can afford to spend a little time in his own apartment. He leaves careful instructions to fetch him *immediately* if his Radiancy emerges from the study. Then he whisks out the back stairs that come out right near his rooms.

Conju's majordomo is, as always, excellent. As soon as he opened the door, she begins herding him into the bathroom. “How long do you have, my lord?” she asks.

“A bell, at most,” he says, knowing she is concerned he doesn't have more. He doesn't bother saying he could be called back any time before then, and she doesn't chide him for sleeping in his workroom. They both know.

Between the bath and the chance to do his full skincare routine, Conju almost feels like himself. He's considering if he can fit in an early lunch, when word comes that his Radiancy has left the study half a bell early and is heading for the workroom.

Conju slips into the workroom. One of the guards has placed a very full looking pack against the wall. On the workbench are the baskets Conju had prepared.

His Radiancy is running his hands through the basket of items that don't require purification. Carefully ignoring the option of 'gleeful', Conju settles on 'curious' to describe his lord's face. It *is* a very odd assortment of objects.

“What's this?” says his Radiancy softly, holding up a small glass lump.

“I believe it is a piece from a board game, my lord, some people carry them for luck.” says Conju, smiling. “Is there anything else that might be useful, my lord?”

His Radiancy looks over the table, and at the pack against the wall. “Would you mind opening that, Conju? I would like to see what it contains.”

“Of course, my lord,” says Conju, and unbuckles the top. He carefully removes each item. There are cupboards along the sides of the room, and Conju would usually arrange schooled magic items along the top. Now he lays the items from the pack out in order along there. There are some items he doesn't recognise, and some that are obvious. He carefully lays the knife at a good distance from everything else. There's a very clever mage light with a cover and a headband it fits inside of. A little folding spoon, a contraption that must be some kind of magic water filtration device, and a tiny stove that looks like it could just about heat the single cup.

Carefully putting both hands behind his back, his Radiancy looks closely at each item. “They fit a lot into the bag, don't they,” he says.

“I suppose, my lord,” says Conju uncertainly. He decides to offer a little more, “When I went camping with Cliopher, my lord, he did not take nearly so much equipment. But then, he was very keen to travel light. Perhaps the guards do not mind the weight, my lord.”

There is a pause. Perhaps Conju shouldn't have mentioned Cliopher.

“Cliopher did not take a sleeping roll? Or a light?” says his Radiancy, gesturing at the items in question.

“No, my lord. Nor a flint and steel or any firestarter, although he did take an oilcloth sheet. And he did take a water filter, he said something about the Liauu not containing enough coconuts,” says Conju, remembering fondly Cliopher promising to teach him how to open a coconut some day.

“No way to start a fire?” says his Radiancy, curious.

“He said, my lord, that he could start a fire in more than twenty different ways, and he wasn't going to waste space. Although he did take a book,” answers Conju.

Something in his lord relaxes slightly. Conju suddenly realises that his Radiancy has been imagining his eminently capable bureaucrat stuck in the wilderness, with only his writing kit and uniform. Getting more and more lost, alone in the woods of Alinor.

“My lord,” says Conju, suddenly realising he can offer his lord much more assurance, “Cliopher is an exceedingly capable wilderness traveller. He can hunt, knows many plants that are safe for eating, can navigate by the stars, and construct his own shelters.” He cuts himself off before he launches into an anecdote about Cliopher insisting he could even make his own clothes.

His Radiancy blinks. “Ah,” he says softly, “I had not put together his stories of what his family had taught him in that way. Thank you Conju, that is... good to know.”

Conju nervously straightens the items he had pulled from the pack. “If there is anything else I can tell you, my lord, that could be useful, please ask.”

“He has been to Alinor before, has he not?” asks his Radiancy.

“Yes, my lord, before the Fall. He had a cousin who was an innkeeper there,” answers Conju, more confidently.

“Basil?” says his Radiancy.

“Yes, my lord. I believe the inn was called The Bee at the Border,” says Conju.

His Radiancy nods, and offers Conju a small smile. “Perhaps he will head there. It used to be near the portal to Astandalas, and very busy. I imagine it is probably quieter now.”

They both stop there. Conju trying to push away his memories of the Fall, and wondering what his Radiancy remembers of that terrible time.

“I am sure Cliopher has already tried to send you a letter, my lord,” says Conju.

His Radiancy smiles slightly, and looks back over the room. “I look forward to seeing his recommendations for improving intermundial post.”
