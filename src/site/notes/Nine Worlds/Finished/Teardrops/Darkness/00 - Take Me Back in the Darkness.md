“Good,” came Lord Igel's voice from somewhere behind Kip. “Sayo Madon, stay where you are until the Glorious One dismisses you.”

“Yes, Lord Igel,” said Kip quietly, partially muffled by the bedspread.

There was the sound of the bed curtains being pulled closed, retreating footsteps, and the door closing. Then, silence.

Kip was in the Imperial Bed, with the Emperor. The curtains were closed, the attendants gone. His tutors had made it very clear what to expect if he was 'fortunate' enough to find himself here. Lord Igel had explained that the Emperor simply entertaining himself by making Kip his secretary.
