He was still there when the priest-wizards, Lord Igel, and today's crowd of observers, arrived for the dawn ceremonies. Nobody paid any attention to Kip, who had lost feeling in both his feet.

As the attendants pulled the bed curtains back, Kip rushed forward to kneel beside the Emperor's slippers. He stumbled a little, as feeling returned painfully to his feet, but made it without too much embarrassment.
