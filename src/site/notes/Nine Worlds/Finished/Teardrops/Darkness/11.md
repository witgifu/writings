Kip woke to the sound of the Emperor crying softly. Thinking to soothe another nightmare, he crawled over to the shining ball.

The Emperor twitched at the sound, rolled over, and scrambled away from him. Tears streaming down his face, his eyes red and puffy.

His heart in his throat, Kip crawled backwards into the full formal prostration. His head was just high enough to see his lord's hand, should he gesture for Kip to leave.

The only sound was the Emperor's ragged breathing. He neither signalled for Kip to rise, nor for him to go. Had his lord had a nightmare, and woken from it? Why did he scramble away? Had Kip done something in his sleep? Should he have stayed awake?

Why was the Imperial Bed the one place he could never do anything right?

Trying to push down his fear, Kip whispered, “Please, my lord, I am so sorry, I beg you, tell me what I have done wrong?”

“Done wrong?” whispered the Emperor, his voice broken from crying, “Done wrong? Cliopher, you are perhaps the only good person in this entire forsaken Palace. _I_ have once again assaulted _you_. I couldn't stop myself! And nobody else even thought to. I'm a monster. Just like my uncle and cousin.”

Despite seeing no gesture to rise, Kip moved just enough that he could see his lord's face. He looked even more scared than last time, tinged with yellow like when he had been ill the day before.

Slowly, as he became more alert, the pieces of the situation started to fit together in Kip's mind. He did not like the shape it was making. “My lord, were you not aware that you had taken an erotic stimulant?”

“An erotic stimulant?” echoed the Emperor, confused.

“They're common at orgies. And some sex workers use them, and often their clients do. I recognised the symptoms, Glorious One, but… I thought… you had taken it deliberately.” Kip got quieter and quieter as he spoke. He had taken advantage of the Emperor while he was under the influence of a mind-altering drug, which Kip had known about, and the Emperor had not. He should report _himself_ for treason.

How could he have done such a thing? He felt sick and dizzy. Multiple times, on multiple occasions, he had raped his Emperor. He didn't stand apart from the crimes of the Empire, he had joined them willingly. He was disgusting. Warped. He should be executed for public safety.

“Someone drugged me?” whispered the Emperor, barely more than a breath, and began to shake.

Kip nodded. But if he was scared, he was clearly not as scared as his lord, who was starting to hyperventilate. Kip put his own fears aside for the moment, and rose to kneeling. He looked at the Emperor, keeping his face calm and his body still. Deliberately, he breathed loudly, and slowly.

“It will be alright, my lord, it's over now,” he said softly, “Breathe with me, slowly, in… out… in… out...”

Kip wanted to move closer, to comfort the Emperor with a hug, or a massage, or just by simply holding his hand. But that would be so wrong. How could he go from hurting him to trying to soothe him, with the same hands? So he stayed where he was, watching as his lord shook and tried to breathe with Kip.

Calling the attendants for the calming medication was definitely _not_ going to be the correct choice.

Had the Emperor also been drugged the previous night? Was this an ongoing attempt by someone? But what could they possibly hope to achieve? They were certainly risking their own life.

The stimulant took a bit of time to activate, but not a long time. It wasn't possible for it to have been given to the Emperor before dinner. Kip remembered the taste of the jam tarts. Those heavy spices could have disguised the taste of the stimulant. It would have been easy to arrange them on the platter to make it more likely that Kip or the Emperor took any particular one.

Slowly, the Emperor's breathing calmed down. Once he stopped shaking, Kip stopped breathing loud enough for the Emperor to see and follow. Now that the Glorious One was out of immediate danger to himself, Kip offered his confession.

“Please, my lord, call the guards and have your servant taken away. I confess that, in full knowledge of my actions, I have violated your Serenity's body, mind, and spirit. Or if you would send your servant away, I will go willingly. I have no desire to be an unwelcome bedfellow or an unwanted intrusion in your life, most merciful lord, and I humbly beg your forgiveness for implying otherwise. You have been always kind and generous towards me, beyond my deserving, and I have repaid you with violence.”

Kip fell back down into the obeisance, not sure what else he could do. As the victim, the Emperor deserved to make his own choices about what happened next. As the Lord Emperor of Astandalas, and the most powerful person in all the nine worlds, it was also entirely his right.

Eventually, the Emperor whispered back, “You still do not ask to be sent home, Cliopher?”

Kip looked up into the Emperor's face, surprised by the question. “My lord, I do not deserve such mercy,” and then turned his face down again. He owed the Emperor an explanation for what he thought 'being sent home' would actually mean. 

“My teachers never said explicitly what would happen if someone was chosen, and then sent away, Illustrious One. But they strongly implied that those who were not executed would be forced into a monastery.”

“Ah,” said the Emperor, and softly sighed, with a hand signal for Kip to rise.

He did so, but with fire in his heart. He wanted the Emperor, his Emperor, to know that he hadn't lied. (He was a rapist, what did lying count against that)

“If I had refused to participate in the training, I would have been sent to hard labour. I chose to do the training. If I have a choice now between sex work and being a cloistered monk, I would prefer sex work. I am far from the only person in the Empire facing uncomfortable choices, and I decided to be grateful that I had a choice when many do not.”

Suddenly remembering who he was talking to, and what he had done, he bowed his head.

“Please call the guards to take me away, most just and gracious lord. I have committed terrible crimes against you.”

Belatedly, Kip wondered how much of this the guards and attendants had heard.

“You did nothing wrong, I won't force you to leave, Cliopher,” whispered his lord, and then almost inaudible, “I don't want you to leave.”

Kip looked up, tears spilling from his eyes. He did not deserve such trust, or forgiveness. He could not understand such mercy. 

“I swear, Glorious One, I will not touch you, without a direct request. If I think you are under the influence of any drug, I will apply first aid and seek medical assistance.”

The Emperor did not react, not positively or negatively. Nervous that he had misjudged the moment, Kip continued.

“I will try to find who did this. I am willing test any food or drink. Although if someone is happy to drug you, Glorious One, I am not sure that I am much of an obstacle.”

The Emperor gestured for him to come closer, and so Kip scrambled up the bed towards where his lord sat. Careful not to touch, but otherwise as close as he could be.

“I do not know anyone in my household or guards that I trust,” breathed the Emperor, barely moving his lips, “Even if you found who drugged me, would my order to dismiss them be followed?”

Kip shook his head. “There are good people here. I will find them.”

The Emperor squeezed his eyes shut. “I'm sorry, Cliopher” he breathed, “I'm sorry I trapped you here with me.” He slowly slid one hand across the sheets towards Kip.

Kip moved his own hand, slowly, giving the Emperor plenty of time to snatch his hand back. He hovered it above his lord's. Then he snatched it back, blushing at almost breaking his promise so quickly. 

“Glorious One, please, what can I do to help you?” he said gently.

The Emperor let out a silent sigh. “By your hands I have had the best sleep since I was crowned.”

Kip smiled a little at this praise. Taking this as a request, he reached out both hands towards his lord's head. There he gently smoothed the furrows, and wiped away the tears.

---

Waking at dawn again, in the Imperial Bed, Kip wondered for a moment if the previous day had been a dream. It certainly felt surreal. He looked around for the Emperor, and saw he was already rising, the curtains pulled back.

As the Emperor strode through the gawping nobles, Kip slid over to the edge of the bed. There he knelt, his eyes firmly on the Emperor. Let them gossip. He had no shame for his position. Only his actions.

When the ritual was completed, rather than heading to the dressing room, the Emperor walked back towards the bed. Kip bowed, as his lord drew near.

“I will see you at breakfast, Cliopher,” said the Emperor, and put his hand on Kip's shoulder.

“Yes, Glorious One,” said Kip, looking up into his lord's face. He had on the full serenity, but just for Kip his eyes were tense with worry.

Kip smiled weakly, trying to project hope. The Emperor turned, and left for the dressing room. As he did so, aware of the eyes of many witnesses, Kip performed his obeisance to the Emperor's back.

Ignoring everyone else, once the Emperor had left, Kip returned to his own rooms. He was not going to visit the pages half-naked.
