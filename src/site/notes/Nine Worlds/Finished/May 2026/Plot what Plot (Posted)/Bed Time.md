Conju opened the door to Cliopher's study. It was well past the midnight bells, and the man hadn't even had the decency to get out of his evening clothes. Instead of settling into bed with a light novel, as Conju had politely hinted on more than one occasion, here he was with a stack of dispatch boxes. Which, if they couldn't wait until morning, could certainly be given to whomever in the Offices worked night shift.

A gently cough brought Cliopher's head up. "My dear," said Conju, "Really? *He* is in bed, and I am heading to my own, and I find you still working."

"I just needed to clear up a few things—" protested Cliopher.

Conju interrupted, "And what time do you think it is?"

Cliopher said, "A bit after—oh," as he looked over at the clock. "I'm very nearly done, it will take extra time if I have to pick it up tomorrow."

By the muleish look on Cliopher's face, Conju could tell he wasn't going to listen to reason. So Conju stepped right up to the desk, and held out a card, pinched between elegant fingers.

Cliopher moved to take it, but Conju tutted. "*He* gave me this.  I'll not let you take it."

Obediently, Cliopher just leaned closer to read it.

"Cliopher Mdang, Go to bed," was all it said, and then the Imperial Seal was stamped at the bottom.

Cliopher looked up in confusion.

"Do you remember when there was that nasty rumour about the Parnod boy? And I helped Rhodin find the source and squash the rumour? Well his Radiancy offered me a reward. I asked for this."

Conju paused, to allow that to sink in. Not jewels, or silks, or even a holiday. 

"It's good for multiple uses," said Conju, "So. Are you going to bed?"

Cliopher stood up, leaving his documents where they were. "Of course," he said, walking around the desk, "Please let his Radiancy know his orders are being followed." He smiled at Conju though, so he wasn't angry.

"Good," said Conju, and sniffed, "I hope I don't have to use it again."
