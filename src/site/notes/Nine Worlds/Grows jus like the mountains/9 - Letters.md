Dearest Conju,

The weather here continues to be warm, but not too hot. I have enjoyed how vigorous the rosemary and sage are growing. They are particularly fine this year, if you would like me to send you any extracts, I would be happy to.

Seldra and Nerisse send their regards, the both of whom have been visiting me which has been an unexpected delight. The latter has lately been much enamoured of the formal dramas of Voonra, and has been parading around quoting lines at everyone. I can only hope this is a short-lived fancy, as it is quite overwrought.

It was only when I told her that you had joined the Service, that Nerisse finally stopped declaiming about the evils of some knight. She and I were both glad to hear you have settled into a stable position. And what a position! I must say, I never expected you to aim so high, or be so interested in politics. Your father and I often worried that one of our children might be drawn into the orbit of the Court, but of course those were very different times. I am most proud to see my little boy doing his duty to the Empire.

I was delighted to hear you had opened the family suite; of course you must. Furthermore, I am sure I said as much when you left, and of course now even more so. Neither I nor your sister have much cause to visit court, and it is better that the rooms be put to good use. Do take the primary bedroom; consider yourself as our family's emissary to the Court. I can see no reason to visit soon, unless it was to see you. We are all much obliged that the Lord Emperor is not one to summon us.

You mentioned a new friend, which does ease my heart, but you gave no name or family. Really, Conju, are they so embarrassing? Or did you simply forget because you are so busy now? You will leave me speculating that you have fallen in with the Red Company, or some other such miscreants.

Sending all our good wishes,
Your loving Mother

Dear Kip,

Yesterday, Ghillian Poyë came to visit mama. She had clearly been crying, and holed up together with mama for hours. She didn't say anything to me, but the only reason I could think she would do that, apart from hearing that you had _died_ is that you _broke up with her_.

You had been together for nearly ten years! I would have sworn you were months away from coming home and (finally!) marrying her. But instead you write her a letter and break it off? I don't know how people handle relationships on Ysthar, but that was _cruel_. I tried to hint that you should have broken it off before leaving, but then you went and got engaged, so I thought maybe you had told her (and not mama, I understand that at least) you would come back in five years or less.

After work, I went out and found your friends Toucan and Cora, and convinced them to come over and see if they could help Ghillian. Neither of them had heard from you recently either. You better write to all of us soon, and explain what you think you are doing. Don't be surprised if you get a very angry letter from mama, you know she loves Ghillian dearly.

Vinyë

P.S.
If mama tells you I'm pregnant, it's still very early, and I'm not quite sure yet.

---

To my dearest son, Kip,

*How could you*! Today I spent three hours comforting poor Ghillian, who showed me a letter *you* apparently wrote to her. I've never seen you turn so cold. What in the nine worlds could have made you do this?

Have you met someone in Astandalas? I had thought you were the kind of boy who took his promises seriously. Ghillian has spent _years_ waiting for you, and now you run off with some _velio_ siren. Will you even come home at all? I should have locked you in your room when you said you were leaving.

I can't imagine *what* I will tell Lazo or Uncle Tovo, who have both been asking me when you will come back. That you have abandoned all your traditions and family, everything we ever taught you?

Mama

---

Kip,

Are you okay? Is there some kind of brain fever going round in Astandalas? Cora told me you broke up with Ghilly, but that can't be right. I know you used to love a good practical joke, but that's way beyond a joke.

If you'd broken it off before you left, I guess I would have understood. But what's happened now? Did you get some big promotion? I can't imagine you met someone else, that doesn't sound like you. But then neither did what you wrote to Ghilly!

Bertie

---

Kip,

After reading over your letter many times, and talking with all our friends, I do believe you mean this for the best. I can't deny it hurt, because of course it did. I _do_ understand why you couldn't make the trip home to tell me. 

Still, I suppose I am in some ways glad it didn't continue. I'm not sure what I would have done if you still hadn't come home after ten years. Everyone has been very kind and supportive. I hope there is some friend in Astandalas (you hinted there might be) to be there for you.

I have enclosed the _efela_ you gave me.

Wishing you all the best,
Ghilly

P.S.
I got the permanent position at the University.
